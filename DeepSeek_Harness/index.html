    Good to know

    • Off-peak = 50% of peak. Peak hours are 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC, Monday–Friday.
    • Weekends are always off-peak — the whole of Saturday and Sunday UTC, all day.
    • Chinese public holidays are off-peak for the full day. Dates follow the State Council calendar and aren't computed here — use the toggle above.
    • Fees are deducted from your granted balance first, then your topped-up balance.
    • Prices can change; DeepSeek reserves the right to adjust them.
